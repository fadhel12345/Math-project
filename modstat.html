What

You're calculating statistical measures from your data. Mean is the average, median is the middle value, mode is the most frequent, and range shows the spread.

Why it matters

These measures help summarize large datasets into a few meaningful numbers. The box plot shows the distribution shape — the box contains the middle 50% of data.

Real-life uses

Statistics are used in sports analytics, medical research, business decisions, weather forecasting, and polling predictions.
