📚 What's happening here?

What

You're running probability experiments. Each trial is random, but as you repeat the experiment many times, the results converge toward the expected probabilities.

Why it matters

This is the Law of Large Numbers — with enough trials, experimental probability approaches theoretical probability. A fair coin should approach 50/50 heads/tails.

Real-life uses

Probability is used in weather forecasts, medical testing, insurance pricing, game design, and stock market analysis.
