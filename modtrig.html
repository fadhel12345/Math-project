Quick Angles

What is this?

The unit circle shows how sin, cos, and tan relate to angles. The point rotates around a circle of radius 1. Sin is the vertical distance, cos is the horizontal distance, and tan is the ratio sin/cos.

Why does it work?

As the angle changes, the projections on the axes create smooth wave patterns. At 90° sin is maximum (1), cos is zero. At 0° it's the opposite. This is why trigonometric functions are periodic.

Real-life uses

Trigonometry is used in GPS navigation, music synthesis, building bridges, computer graphics, and game physics.
