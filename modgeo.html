What is this?

You're manipulating geometric shapes and seeing how their measurements change in real-time. Drag the points to resize and reshape them.

Why does it work?

Area measures the space inside a shape, perimeter measures the boundary length. Different formulas apply to different shapes: triangle uses Heron's formula, rectangle uses length × width, and circle uses πr².

Real-life uses

Geometry is essential in architecture, land surveying, art, interior design, packaging, and manufacturing.
