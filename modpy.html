Famous Triples

What

You're seeing the Pythagorean theorem visually. The squares drawn on each side of the right triangle show that the area of the two smaller squares always equals the area of the largest square.

Why

This works because of how right triangles relate to squares. The hypotenuse (longest side) always satisfies a² + b² = c². This is one of the most fundamental theorems in all of mathematics.

Real Life

Used in construction, navigation, GPS calculations, computer graphics, and finding distances on maps.
